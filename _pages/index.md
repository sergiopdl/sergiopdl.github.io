I'm an interdisciplinary researcher with a PhD in <a href="https://cogsci.ucmerced.edu/graduate-programs/prospective-graduate-students/phd-program" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cognitive and Information Sciences</a> and 13+ years of product design and development experience.
